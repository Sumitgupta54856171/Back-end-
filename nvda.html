Our Process

Precision in every frame

We don't just animate pixels. We engineer motion systems that scale, perform, and convert. Our methodology blends creative direction with technical architecture.

01 — Discovery & Strategy
Audit existing assets, define motion principles, establish technical constraints & KPIs.
02 — Motion Design Systems
Design token-driven animation specs: easing curves, duration scales, choreography rules.
03 — Prototyping & Dev Handoff
Interactive Framer/Code prototypes. Lottie/Rive/GSAP production builds. Zero guesswork.
04 — Implementation & QA
Developer embedding, performance profiling (INP/CLS), cross-browser testing, documentation.

About Axiom

A small team.
Massive impact.

Founded in 2018, Axiom operates at the intersection of design, engineering, and strategy. We partner with funded startups and enterprise brands to build motion design systems that ship.

Our core belief: Motion is not decoration. It is communication. Every micro-interaction, page transition, and data visualization serves a cognitive purpose — reducing load, guiding attention, and reinforcing brand personality.

We write our own tools. We contribute to open source. We speak at conferences. We treat every project as R&D for the next one.
